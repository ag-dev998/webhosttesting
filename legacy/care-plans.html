FAQ

Questions about care plans

Why is a care plan required?

A website isn't a one-time purchase. It needs hosting, security updates, backups and someone watching that your leads actually arrive. I only build sites I also look after, so they don't quietly break after launch.

Can I make changes myself?

If your site has an edit screen (included in the Growth package), you can update hours, services and photos yourself. On any plan, you can just email me and I'll handle it within your plan's response time.

Can I switch plans?

Yes. You can move up or down a plan at the start of any month. [Confirm policy.]

What if I want to cancel?

After the [6 / 12]-month minimum term, plans are month-to-month with 30 days' written notice. [Confirm against your contract.]

I have a website someone else built. Can I just get a care plan?

Possibly. I'll take a look at how it's built and let you know if I can support it, or if a rebuild would cost less over time.
