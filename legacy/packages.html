FAQ

Questions about the build

How long does it take?

Launch sites usually go live in 2–3 weeks and Growth sites in 4–6 weeks. The biggest factor is how quickly we get your content (logo, photos, service details), so I'll send you a simple checklist on day one.

How does payment work?

50% deposit to start and 50% when your site goes live. Your care plan starts the month the site launches.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Once it's paid in full, your content, your domain and your finished site are yours. If you ever leave, I'll hand everything over.

I already have a website. Can you help?

Yes. Most projects start by replacing a slow or outdated site. I'll make sure your important pages redirect so you keep your Google rankings.

Not sure which package fits?

Tell me a bit about your business and I'll recommend one. No pressure, no jargon.
